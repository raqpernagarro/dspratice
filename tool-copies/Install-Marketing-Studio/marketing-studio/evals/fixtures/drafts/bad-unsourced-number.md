A redesign is easy to celebrate and hard to explain afterwards.

Our last redesign cut support tickets by 40%.

What I remember better is the form where people kept getting stuck. We had added a field for a good reason, and nobody could tell what it was for. We changed one sentence of help text, and the tickets about it stopped.

Before your next redesign, pick one support ticket that arrives every week and ask what the product could say differently.
