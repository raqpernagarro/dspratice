Nobody ships an off-brand component on purpose.

It happens one small decision at a time.

---

Agents do not guess at random. They fill each gap in your system with the most likely answer, and the most likely answer looks right in review. That is why the drift is hard to see: every single decision is defensible, and none of them was ever made by anyone on the team. Nobody decided any of this.

---

The fix sits upstream, in what the system tells the agent before it starts.
