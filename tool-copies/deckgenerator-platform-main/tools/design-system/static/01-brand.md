# Brand and principles

## What a Nagarro slide is

A Nagarro slide is a page from a well-made report, not a poster. It is restrained, editorial and structural: a strict 1920×1080 frame, a tight type scale, generous negative space and one persistent frame (the header and footer). Hierarchy comes from scale, weight and space. Nothing is decoration for its own sake.

## Principles

- **Structure first.** Every slide sits in the same frame: header at the top margin, content from y=156, footer at the bottom margin. Consistency across 30 slides is what makes the deck read as one argument.
- **One idea per slide.** The title states it; the body supports it. If a slide needs two titles, it is two slides.
- **One accent.** Mint green appears once per slide, on purpose: the key number, the highlighted phrase, the active step, a scribble. Everything else is ink and white.
- **Real content.** Real numbers with units, real names and roles, real photographs from the library. A template's example text is a placeholder, never a deliverable.
- **Caring, not loud.** The brand describes itself as a caring brand: warm photography, plain language, no hype.

## The surfaces

| Surface | Tokens | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light slide | `bg-primary` #ffffff, `fg-100` #010716 | Every content slide |
| Dark slide | `bg-primary` = black/100 #010716, `fg-100` white | Cover, chapter dividers, closing, the occasional statement |

A deck alternates them as a sandwich: dark open, light content, dark breaks between chapters, dark close.

## The caring values

Nagarro names seven values, each with its own colour and icon: **Global** #13294b, **Caring** #2d807b, **Client-centric** #2e008b, **Intelligent** #6240a8, **Non-hierarchical** #f8485e, **Agile** #f99068 and **Responsible** #06041f. These are brand colours from the caring-icon artwork, not Figma variables. Use them and the caring icons (Assets › Caring) only when the content is about the values themselves: a culture slide, a values wall, a recruiting deck. They are not a chart palette. Among the masters, only `portfolio/grid-21` uses them.

## The logo

On slides the logo comes with the Header: the mint mark with the wordmark in `fg-100`, so it reads black on light slides and white on dark ones. For anything outside a slide, use the official lockups in Assets › Logos (Horizontal, Vertical, Tagline, Small; Dark, Light, White, Green, Petrol Black). The "Light" lockups go on light grounds, the "Dark" lockups on dark grounds. Never redraw, stretch, recolour, rotate or add effects to the logo.
