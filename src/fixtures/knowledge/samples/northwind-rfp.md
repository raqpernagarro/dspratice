# Request for proposal: AI-native design system programme

**Northwind Retail · Digital Product Office** (sample request for the Nagarro hub; Northwind is fictional)
Responses are due by 14 November 2026. Please send a written response to each question below.

## Background

Northwind runs a web shop, a store-operations app and a loyalty app, built by four product teams. We have a component library in Figma and a React library in code, but they drift apart, and nobody owns the system full time. Our teams started using Copilot and Claude to build screens this year. We want to make sure the UI they generate stays on-brand and accessible.

We want to modernise our design system so that people and AI tools build from one shared foundation, and so that our own teams can run it after the engagement.

## Requirements and questions

1. Describe how you would assess the current state of our design system, in Figma and in code, including how ready it is for AI tools.
2. What deliverables do we receive at the end of the assessment?
3. Explain the governance model you recommend for changes to tokens and shared components, including who approves changes proposed by AI tools.
4. How do you keep Figma and code in sync over time?
5. How will your approach make our design guidelines readable by AI coding tools?
6. Describe how you would enable our designers and engineers to own and extend the system after handover (training, coaching, documentation).
7. How do you make sure AI-generated UI meets WCAG 2.2 AA?
8. Provide your pricing, including day rates and the total cost for each phase.
9. Provide two references from comparable clients that we can contact.
10. Include CVs of the proposed team members.
11. State your SLA for support after go-live.
12. Confirm the earliest start date and the team availability.
13. Our store-operations app is built in Angular. Confirm that your approach supports Angular components.
14. How do you measure whether the design system is working after the engagement?

## Evaluation

We will evaluate responses on approach, experience with AI-ready design systems, and how well the proposal enables our own teams.
